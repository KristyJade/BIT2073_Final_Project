                 WHACK FOOD CORNER - REDESIGN PREVIEW

Welcome to the Whack Food Corner website redesign preview!

HOW TO OPEN & VIEW THIS WEBSITE:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Extract all contents of WHACK-FOOD-CORNER-PREVIEW.zip if you haven't already.
2. Double-click on "index.html" to open the website in your favorite web browser 
   (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, Brave, etc.).
3. No local server, node.js, or complex installation is required.

PAGES INCLUDED IN THIS PREVIEW:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Home Page (index.html) - Scroll parallax hero, category highlights, about split card.
- Craft Menu (menu.html) - Restaurant menu sheet with category listings & pricing.
- Online Ordering (order.html) - Wix Restaurants ordering interface with item cards.
- My Cart (cart.html) - Interactive cart with quantity controls, promo code, and order summary.
- Checkout & Payment (checkout.html) - Payment method selector and order confirmation step.
- My Orders (orders.html) - Live order status tracking (#WFC-89241 Preparing Kitchen) & history.
- Sign Up & Login (login.html) - Branded authentication experience with social sign-in.
- Account Settings (account.html) - Member portal tabs and personal info management.
- About Us (about.html) - Restaurant brand story and quality pillars.
- Contact & Location (contact.html) - PJ outlet details, operating hours, and inquiry form.
- Floating Live Chat - Click "Let's Chat!" at the bottom right of any page to test live support.

TECHNICAL NOTE FOR REVIEW:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
This package contains a front-end design prototype created for client visual review. 
It demonstrates the exact visual system, typography, motion, responsiveness, and customer flow 
designed for Wix.com platform implementation. Real database authentication, Wix Restaurants back-end, 
and payment processing remain on the live Wix platform.

Enjoy testing the website!
